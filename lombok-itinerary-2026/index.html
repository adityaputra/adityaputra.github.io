Food, shopping & places

  • Traditional Sasak village
    Sade or Ende — best fitted on Friday's southbound drive.
  • Tanjung Aan + Bukit Merese
    Picturesque south-coast stop before Novotel. Skip exposed hill walks in strong sun.

    Local snacks, textiles, shirts and gifts. Optional on the way south or the return route, depending on time.
  • Ayam taliwang and plecing
    You've already visited Ayam Taliwang Irama 3; other local specialties include sate rembiga.

    Good for a coastal day; agree on prices before ordering.
